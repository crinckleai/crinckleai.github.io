### Pillar 3: AI-Powered Supply Review

| Process Step | Traditional | AI-Automated |
|--------------|-------------|--------------|
| Capacity Planning | Spreadsheet calculations | AI optimization solver |
| Inventory Optimization | Rule-based targets | ML-driven dynamic targets |
| Supplier Management | Manual monitoring | AI risk scoring + alerts |
| Constraint Resolution | Meeting discussions | AI recommends optimal solutions |
| Network Optimization | Annual consultants | Continuous AI optimization |

**AI Automation Points:**
1. **Auto Safety Stock**: ML calculates optimal SS based on service level and variability
2. **Capacity Optimizer**: AI solver balances cost, service, utilization
3. **Supplier Risk AI**: Monitors financial, geopolitical, weather risks real-time
4. **Smart Allocation**: AI allocates constrained supply to maximize margin/service
5. **Network Digital Twin**: AI simulates network changes before implementation

**Inventory Optimization Formula (Auto-Calculated):**
```
Safety Stock = Z × √(LT × σD² + D² × σLT²)

Where AI automatically:
• Calculates Z from target service level
• Measures demand variability (σD) from history
• Tracks lead time variability (σLT) from receipts
• Updates daily based on latest data
```
