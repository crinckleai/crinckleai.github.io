## Executive Overview

### What is Integrated Business Planning (IBP)?

Integrated Business Planning (IBP) is an evolved, enterprise-wide planning process that extends traditional Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) by integrating strategic, financial, and operational plans into a single, cohesive framework. For B2B enterprises like SIG, IBP serves as the critical bridge between long-term strategy and day-to-day execution.

### The Business Case for IBP in B2B

| Benefit Category | Typical Improvement Range |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Forecast Accuracy | 20-50% improvement |
| Inventory Reduction | 15-30% reduction |
| Customer Service Levels | 5-15% improvement |
| Revenue Growth | 2-5% increase |
| Working Capital Optimization | 10-25% improvement |
| Planning Cycle Time | 50-75% reduction |

### IBP vs. Traditional S&OP

| Aspect | Traditional S&OP | Integrated Business Planning |
|--------|------------------|------------------------------|
| Horizon | 12-18 months | 24-36+ months |
| Focus | Supply-demand balancing | Strategy-to-execution alignment |
| Financial Integration | Limited | Full P&L and balance sheet |
| Scope | Operations-centric | Enterprise-wide |
| Decision Authority | Middle management | Executive leadership |
| Scenario Planning | Basic | Advanced, multi-dimensional |

## The Five Core IBP Pillars

### Pillar 1: Product Management Review

**Objective:** Align product portfolio with market opportunities and strategic objectives.

#### Best Practices:

1. **Portfolio Lifecycle Management**
   - Maintain rolling 24-month product roadmap
   - Establish stage-gate process with clear go/no-go criteria
   - Track portfolio health metrics (revenue mix, margin contribution, vitality index)

2. **New Product Introduction (NPI) Integration**
   - Integrate NPI timelines into demand and supply planning
   - Define clear volume ramp assumptions
   - Establish cannibalization impact assessments

3. **Product Rationalization**
   - Regular SKU complexity analysis
   - Tail management with clear sunset criteria
   - Cost-to-serve evaluation for low-volume products

#### B2B Specific Considerations:

- **Customer Co-Development:** Incorporate joint development timelines with key accounts
- **Technical Specifications:** Align engineering changes with customer qualification cycles
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Track certification requirements across markets

#### Key Metrics:

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| Portfolio Vitality Index | >25% revenue from products <3 years | Monthly |
| NPI Success Rate | >70% | Quarterly |
| SKU Rationalization | 10-15% tail reduction annually | Quarterly |
| Time-to-Market | Industry benchmark -20% | Per launch |

---

### Pillar 2: Demand Review

**Objective:** Generate accurate, unbiased demand signals that drive enterprise planning.

#### Best Practices:

1. **Statistical Baseline Forecasting**
   - Deploy appropriate algorithms based on demand patterns
   - Implement automated model selection
   - Maintain clean historical data with outlier management

2. **Demand Sensing & Shaping**
   - Near-term sensing using leading indicators
   - Promotional and pricing impact modeling
   - Market intelligence integration

3. **Consensus Demand Process**
   - Structured collaboration between Sales, Marketing, and Finance
   - Clear assumption documentation
   - Bias tracking and accountability

4. **Segmented Planning Approach**
   - ABC-XYZ segmentation for planning intensity
   - Customer/channel-specific forecasting
   - Product family vs. SKU level planning

#### B2B Specific Considerations:

- **Customer Collaboration:** Joint forecasting with strategic accounts
- **Contract Manufacturing:** Incorporate contractual commitments
- **Project-Based Demand:** Separate project forecasting from base demand
- **Long Lead-Time Items:** Extended horizon planning for engineered products

#### Demand Planning Hierarchy:

```
Level 1: Strategic (24-36 months)
├── Market/Region aggregation
├── Product family groupings
└── Financial alignment focus

Level 2: Tactical (3-18 months)
├── Customer segment detail
├── SKU family planning
└── Capacity planning focus

Level 3: Operational (0-13 weeks)
├── Customer/SKU detail
├── Order-level visibility
└── Execution focus
```

#### Key Metrics:

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| Forecast Accuracy (Weighted) | >75% at SKU-Location | Monthly |
| Forecast Bias | ±3% | Monthly |
| Demand Plan Adherence | >90% | Monthly |
| Value-Added Forecast | >10% vs. statistical | Quarterly |

### Pillar 3: Supply Review

**Objective:** Develop optimal supply response that balances service, cost, and capital.

#### Best Practices:

1. **Capacity Planning Excellence**
   - Rough-cut capacity planning at aggregate level
   - Demonstrated capacity vs. theoretical tracking
   - Bottleneck identification and management

2. **Inventory Strategy Optimization**
   - Service-level driven inventory targets
   - Multi-echelon inventory optimization
   - Strategic buffer positioning

3. **Supply Network Design**
   - Regular network optimization reviews
   - Make-vs-buy analysis integration
   - Supplier capacity alignment

4. **Scenario-Based Planning**
   - Multiple supply scenarios for demand uncertainty
   - Risk mitigation strategy development
   - Contingency planning protocols

#### B2B Specific Considerations:

- **Custom Manufacturing:** Capacity allocation for engineered-to-order products
- **Supplier Integration:** Collaborative planning with tier-1 suppliers
- **Quality Requirements:** Compliance and certification constraints
- **Capital Equipment:** Long lead-time asset planning

### Pillar 4: Financial Review (Integrated Reconciliation)

**Objective:** Translate operational plans into financial outcomes and ensure alignment with business targets.

#### Best Practices:

1. **Plan-to-Budget Reconciliation**
   - Monthly gap analysis between operating plan and budget
   - Rolling forecast integration
   - Variance explanation and action planning

2. **Scenario Financial Modeling**
   - P&L impact of demand/supply scenarios
   - Working capital implications
   - Investment decision support

3. **One Number Philosophy**
   - Single version of truth across functions
   - Elimination of functional planning silos
   - Aligned incentives and accountability

4. **Gap Closure Management**
   - Structured approach to addressing shortfalls
   - Initiative tracking and accountability
   - Risk-adjusted planning

#### B2B Specific Considerations:

- **Contract Profitability:** Customer and contract-level margin analysis
- **Transfer Pricing:** Multi-entity financial consolidation
- **Currency Exposure:** FX impact on international operations
- **Capital Projects:** Major investment alignment

#### Financial Integration Model:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  VOLUME PLAN (Units)                       │
│  Demand Plan × Product Mix = Unit Volume by SKU           │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                           │
                           ▼
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  REVENUE PLAN                              │
│  Volume × Price/Mix × FX = Gross Revenue                  │
│  - Discounts, Rebates, Returns = Net Revenue              │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                           │
                           ▼
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  MARGIN PLAN                               │
│  Revenue - COGS (Material + Labor + Overhead) = Gross Margin│
│  - Operating Expenses = Operating Income                   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                           │
                           ▼
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  WORKING CAPITAL PLAN                      │
│  Inventory Investment + Receivables - Payables            │
│  = Net Working Capital Requirements                        │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Key Metrics:

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| Plan vs. Actual Revenue | ±3% | Monthly |
| Gross Margin Achievement | ≥Budget | Monthly |
| Working Capital % Revenue | Industry benchmark | Monthly |
| Forecast-to-Budget Gap | Closed within 90 days | Monthly |

### Pillar 5: Management Business Review (Executive IBP)

**Objective:** Executive decision-making forum that resolves conflicts and commits resources.

#### Best Practices:

1. **Structured Decision Framework**
   - Pre-defined decision categories and authority levels
   - Clear escalation criteria
   - Time-bound decision protocols

2. **Exception-Based Management**
   - Focus on gaps and risks, not routine operations
   - Dashboard-driven meeting preparation
   - Limited presentation, maximum discussion

3. **Commitment and Accountability**
   - Executive sign-off on integrated plan
   - Clear ownership of gap closure actions
   - Performance consequence management

4. **Strategic Alignment**
   - Regular strategy refresh and plan alignment
   - Portfolio investment decisions
   - Market and competitive response

#### Meeting Structure Best Practices:

```
Executive IBP Meeting Agenda (90-120 minutes max)

1. Performance Review (15 min)
   - Key metrics vs. targets
   - Root cause of significant variances

2. Plan Review (30 min)
   - Rolling 24-month demand/supply outlook
   - Financial projection vs. targets
   - Key assumptions and risks

3. Gap Analysis & Scenarios (30 min)
   - Identification of plan-to-target gaps
   - Scenario options with trade-offs
   - Risk mitigation strategies

4. Decisions Required (30 min)
   - Investment/divestment decisions
   - Capacity commitments
   - Policy changes
   - Resource allocation

5. Action Items & Commitments (15 min)
   - Clear owners and deadlines
   - Communication requirements
   - Next meeting preparation
```

#### Key Metrics:

| Metric | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| Decision Cycle Time | <48 hours for escalations | Per meeting |
| Meeting Effectiveness Score | >4/5 | Monthly survey |
| Action Item Completion | >90% | Monthly |
| Plan Stability | <10% change month-over-month | Monthly |

### Appendix C: Glossary of IBP Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Bias** | Systematic tendency to over or under forecast |
| **Consensus Demand** | Agreed demand plan after cross-functional review |
| **Constrained Plan** | Supply plan reflecting actual capacity constraints |
| **Demand Sensing** | Short-term demand signal improvement using leading indicators |
| **Demand Shaping** | Actions to influence demand (pricing, promotion, allocation) |
| **MAPE** | Mean Absolute Percentage Error - forecast accuracy measure |
| **NPI** | New Product Introduction |
| **OTIF** | On-Time In-Full - customer service metric |
| **Rough-Cut Capacity** | Aggregate capacity check at product family level |
| **S&OP** | Sales and Operations Planning - IBP predecessor |
| **Safety Stock** | Buffer inventory to protect against variability |
| **SKU** | Stock Keeping Unit - unique product identifier |
| **Statistical Forecast** | Algorithm-generated baseline forecast |
| **Unconstrained Demand** | Demand plan without supply limitations |
| **VMI** | Vendor Managed Inventory |
| **WMAPE** | Weighted Mean Absolute Percentage Error |
