# ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS

## Slide Transitions
| Transition | Type | Duration |
|------------|------|----------|
| Title to Content | Reveal from center | 0.8s |
| Content slides | Elegant fade | 0.6s |
| Decision slides | Attention push | 0.5s |
| Closing | Zoom to center | 1.0s |

## Element Animations
| Element | Animation | Trigger |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Health score | Fill + number count | On appear |
| Risk cards | Fade in position | Staggered |
| Decision boxes | Slide in + glow | Sequential |
| Progress bars | Smooth fill | On appear |
| Commitment seal | Stamp effect | On close |

## Executive Presentation Tips
- Keep slides to 2-3 minutes each max
- Use builds for decision discussion
- Have backup detail slides ready
- Focus on exceptions, not routine
- End with clear asks and commitments
