## Design Guidelines

**Color Coding:**
- Green (G): On track, meeting target
- Yellow (Y): At risk, monitor closely
- Red (R): Off track, action required

**Status Indicators:**
- ↑ Improving trend
- → Stable trend
- ↓ Declining trend
