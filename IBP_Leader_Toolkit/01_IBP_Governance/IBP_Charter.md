# IBP Charter Template
## Integrated Business Planning Process Charter

---

## 1. Purpose & Objectives

### 1.1 Purpose Statement
Integrated Business Planning (IBP) is the enterprise-wide planning process that aligns strategic, financial, and operational plans to optimize business performance and achieve corporate objectives.

### 1.2 Key Objectives
| Objective | Description | Success Measure |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Strategic Alignment | Bridge strategy to execution | Plan supports 3-year strategic goals |
| One Number Plan | Single integrated plan across functions | Zero conflicting plans |
| Financial Integration | Operating plan = Financial forecast | <3% variance to budget |
| Decision Velocity | Timely resolution of business issues | <48hr decision cycle |
| Performance Excellence | Achieve operational KPIs | Top quartile industry performance |

## 4. Process Overview

### 4.1 Monthly Cycle
```
Week 1: Product Review
    ↓
Week 2: Demand Review
    ↓
Week 3: Supply Review
    ↓
Week 4: Financial Integration → Executive IBP
```

### 4.2 Review Descriptions

| Review | Owner | Purpose | Key Outputs |
|--------|-------|---------|-------------|
| Product Review | VP Product/Marketing | Portfolio alignment | Updated product roadmap, NPI status |
| Demand Review | VP Sales/Commercial | Consensus demand | Unconstrained demand plan |
| Supply Review | VP Supply Chain/Ops | Supply response | Constrained supply plan, scenarios |
| Financial Review | VP Finance/FP&A | Financial integration | P&L projection, gap analysis |
| Executive IBP | CEO/COO | Decision & commitment | Approved plan, resource decisions |
