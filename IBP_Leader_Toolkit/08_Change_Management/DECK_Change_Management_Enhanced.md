# ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS

## Slide Transitions
| Transition | Type | Duration |
|------------|------|----------|
| Title to Content | Sunrise reveal | 1.0s |
| Journey slides | Smooth morph | 0.6s |
| Standard content | Fade | 0.5s |
| Celebration | Confetti pop | 0.8s |

## Element Animations
| Element | Animation | Trigger |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| ADKAR boxes | Sequential reveal | Staggered 0.3s |
| Roadmap timeline | Path draw | Left to right |
| Adoption bars | Grow fill | Sequential |
| Win cards | Slide up + glow | On appear |
| Radar chart | Sweep draw | On appear |

## Emotional Design Principles
- Use warm colors to convey support
- Include human stories and quotes
- Celebrate progress, not just completion
- Acknowledge resistance respectfully
- Focus on the "why" and "what's in it for me"
