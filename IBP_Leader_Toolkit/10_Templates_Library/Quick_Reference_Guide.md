# IBP Quick Reference Guide

A one-page reference for IBP participants and leaders.

---

## Monthly IBP Cycle

| Week | Review | Key Activities | Key Outputs |
|------|--------|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | Product Review | Portfolio analysis, NPI status, rationalization | Product decisions, NPI demand |
| 2 | Demand Review | Statistical forecast, overlays, consensus | Consensus demand plan |
| 3 | Supply Review | Capacity check, constraints, scenarios | Constrained supply plan |
| 4 | Financial + Executive | P&L integration, gap analysis, decisions | Approved integrated plan |

---

## Key Deadlines

| Item | Deadline |
|------|----------|

## Meeting Quick Reference

### Product Review (Week 1)
**Duration:** 60-90 min
**Attendees:** Product Management, Marketing, R&D, Sales
**Decisions:** NPI gates, rationalization, portfolio priorities

### Demand Review (Week 2)
**Duration:** 60-90 min
**Attendees:** Sales, Marketing, Demand Planning, Finance
**Decisions:** Consensus demand approval, assumption validation

### Supply Review (Week 3)
**Duration:** 60-90 min
**Attendees:** Supply Chain, Operations, Procurement, Demand Planning
**Decisions:** Supply response, capacity actions, inventory strategy

### Financial Review (Week 4)
**Duration:** 60 min
**Attendees:** Finance, FP&A, IBP Leader
**Decisions:** Financial integration, gap closure actions

### Executive IBP (Week 4)
**Duration:** 90-120 min
**Attendees:** C-Suite, VPs, IBP Leader
**Decisions:** Plan approval, escalated issues, resource allocation

## Scenario Planning

**Standard Scenarios:**
- **Base Case:** Most likely outcome
- **Upside (+10-20%):** What goes right
- **Downside (-10-20%):** What goes wrong

**Decision Triggers:**
- Order backlog > threshold → Upside actions
- Cancellations > threshold → Downside actions
- Lead indicator change → Review scenarios

## Key Principles

1. **One Number** - Single integrated plan across all functions
2. **Forward Looking** - Focus on 24+ month horizon
3. **Decision Focused** - Meetings drive decisions, not presentations
4. **Exception Based** - Focus on gaps and issues
5. **Accountable** - Clear ownership with consequences
6. **Continuous Improvement** - Always getting better

## Quick Formulas

**Forecast Accuracy (MAPE):**
```
1 - (|Actual - Forecast| / Actual) × 100
```

**Forecast Bias:**
```
(Actual - Forecast) / Actual × 100
```

**Days of Supply:**
```
Inventory / Daily Demand
```

**Inventory Turns:**
```
Annual COGS / Average Inventory
```

**OTIF:**
```
Orders On-Time AND In-Full / Total Orders × 100
```
