# IBP Glossary of Terms

A comprehensive reference of Integrated Business Planning terminology.

---

## A

**ABC Analysis**
Classification of items based on importance, typically by revenue or volume. A items are highest value (often 80% of value, 20% of items), B items are medium, and C items are lowest.

**ABC-XYZ Segmentation**
Combining ABC (value) with XYZ (variability) classification to create planning segments with different forecasting and inventory approaches.

**ADKAR Model**
Change management framework: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement.

**Aggregate Planning**
Planning at summarized levels (product family, region) rather than detailed SKU level, typically used for capacity and strategic planning.

**Allocation**
Process of distributing limited supply across customers or channels when demand exceeds availability.

---

## B

**Baseline Forecast**
Statistical forecast generated from historical data before any manual adjustments or overlays.

**Bias**
Systematic tendency to over-forecast (positive bias) or under-forecast (negative bias). Calculated as (Actual - Forecast) / Actual.

**Bill of Materials (BOM)**
Hierarchical structure showing components and quantities required to produce a finished product.

**Bottleneck**
Resource or process step that limits overall throughput or capacity.

**Buffer Stock**
See Safety Stock.

---

## C

**Capacity Planning**
Process of determining production capability required to meet demand, including rough-cut (aggregate) and detailed levels.

**Cannibalization**
Reduction in sales of existing products due to introduction of new products.

**Collaborative Planning, Forecasting, and Replenishment (CPFR)**
Industry standard for supply chain collaboration between trading partners.

**Consensus Demand**
Agreed demand plan resulting from cross-functional review and alignment, incorporating statistical baseline and business intelligence.

**Constrained Plan**
Supply plan that reflects actual capacity and material constraints, as opposed to unconstrained demand.

**Cycle Stock**
Inventory held to meet normal demand between replenishment orders.

---

## D

**Days of Supply (DOS)**
Inventory metric measuring how many days of demand the current inventory can satisfy. Calculated as Inventory / Daily Demand.

**Demand Plan**
Forward-looking projection of customer demand, typically in units and revenue.

**Demand Sensing**
Use of near-term signals (orders, POS data, etc.) to improve short-term forecast accuracy.

**Demand Shaping**
Actions taken to influence demand, such as promotions, pricing changes, or product substitution.

**Demonstrated Capacity**
Actual proven output capability based on historical performance, versus theoretical capacity.

---

## E

**Exception Management**
Focus on items or situations that deviate from plan or targets, rather than reviewing all data.

**Executive IBP (Management Business Review)**
Monthly executive forum for reviewing integrated plan, making decisions, and committing resources.

---

## F

**Fill Rate**
Percentage of demand satisfied from available inventory. Calculated as Units Shipped / Units Ordered.

**Finished Goods Inventory (FGI)**
Inventory of completed products ready for sale or shipment.

**Firm Zone**
Time period within which the production schedule is fixed and changes are not permitted without formal approval.

**Forecast Accuracy**
Measure of how close forecasts are to actual demand. Common metrics include MAPE, WMAPE, and Bias.

**Forecast Horizon**
Time period covered by the forecast, typically 24-36 months for IBP.

**Forecast Value Add (FVA)**
Improvement in forecast accuracy from human judgment/overlays versus statistical baseline alone.

---

## G

**Gap Analysis**
Comparison of current plan to targets (budget, goals) to identify and quantify shortfalls.

**Gap Closure**
Initiatives and actions to address identified gaps between plan and targets.

---

## H

**Horizon**
Time period covered by a plan or forecast. IBP typically uses 24-36 month horizon with more detail in near-term.

---

## I

**Integrated Business Planning (IBP)**
Enterprise-wide planning process that integrates strategic, operational, and financial plans to align the organization on one plan.

**Inventory Turns**
Measure of how many times inventory is sold and replaced in a period. Calculated as COGS / Average Inventory.

---

## K

**Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**
Quantifiable metric used to evaluate performance against objectives.

---

## L

**Lead Time**
Total time from order placement to receipt of goods, or from production start to completion.

**Level Loading**
Smoothing production across time periods to optimize resource utilization and reduce costs.

---

## M

**Make-to-Order (MTO)**
Production strategy where items are manufactured after customer order is received.

**Make-to-Stock (MTS)**
Production strategy where items are manufactured to inventory in anticipation of demand.

**Management Business Review (MBR)**
See Executive IBP.

**MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)**
Common forecast accuracy metric: Average of |Actual - Forecast| / Actual.

**Master Data**
Core reference data shared across systems, including product, customer, and supplier data.

**Material Requirements Planning (MRP)**
System for calculating material needs based on demand, BOM, and inventory.

**Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization (MEIO)**
Optimizing inventory across multiple levels of the supply chain simultaneously.

---

## N

**Net Requirements**
Quantity needed after subtracting available inventory and scheduled receipts from gross requirements.

**New Product Introduction (NPI)**
Process of bringing new products to market, including development, launch, and ramp-up.

---

## O

**On-Time In-Full (OTIF)**
Customer service metric measuring percentage of orders delivered complete and on the promised date.

**Operating Plan**
Forward-looking plan covering demand, supply, and resources, typically expressed in units and converted to financial terms.

**Overlay**
Manual adjustment to statistical forecast based on business intelligence (sales input, marketing plans, etc.).

---

## P

**Perfect Order**
Order delivered on-time, in-full, with correct documentation and no damage. Perfect Order Rate measures percentage of orders meeting all criteria.

**Pipeline Inventory**
Inventory in transit between locations.

**Plan Adherence**
Measure of actual performance versus planned, for either demand (shipments vs. forecast) or supply (production vs. plan).

**Plan Stability**
Measure of how much the plan changes from one period to the next. Lower is generally better.

**Planning Hierarchy**
Structure for aggregating and disaggregating plans, from strategic (aggregate) to operational (detailed).

**Portfolio Management**
Process of managing the mix of products to optimize business performance.

**Product Lifecycle Management (PLM)**
Managing products through stages from introduction to decline/discontinuation.

**Product Review**
IBP meeting focused on portfolio strategy, NPI, and product rationalization.

---

## R

**RACI Matrix**
Tool defining Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed parties for activities.

**Rationalization**
Process of reducing product portfolio complexity by eliminating low-value SKUs.

**Rolling Forecast**
Forecast that extends the planning horizon each period (e.g., always 18 months out).

**Rough-Cut Capacity Planning (RCCP)**
High-level capacity check at aggregate product/resource level.

---

## S

**Safety Stock**
Inventory held to protect against variability in demand and/or supply.

**Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP)**
Precursor to IBP, focused primarily on demand-supply balancing over 12-18 month horizon.

**Scenario Planning**
Developing multiple versions of the plan to prepare for different possible futures.

**Service Level**
Target percentage of demand to be satisfied from inventory (e.g., 95% service level).

**Single Source of Truth**
One agreed version of data that all functions use for planning and decision-making.

**SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**
Unique identifier for a distinct product variant.

**Statistical Forecast**
Forecast generated by algorithms based on historical demand patterns.

**Supply Plan**
Plan showing how demand will be met through production, inventory, and procurement.

**Supply Review**
IBP meeting focused on capacity, inventory, constraints, and supply response.

---

## T

**Tail Management**
Managing low-volume SKUs (the "tail" of the distribution) that add complexity but little value.

**Time Fence**
Point in the planning horizon where rules change (e.g., firm zone, planning zone).

**Tracking Signal**
Cumulative forecast error measure used to detect bias; triggers review when exceeds control limit.

---

## U

**Unconstrained Demand**
Demand forecast without consideration of supply limitations; represents true market demand.

---

## V

**Value Stream**
End-to-end flow of activities that create value for the customer.

**Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI)**
Arrangement where supplier manages customer's inventory levels.

**Vitality Index**
Measure of portfolio health; typically percentage of revenue from products introduced within recent period (e.g., 3 years).

---

## W

**WMAPE (Weighted Mean Absolute Percentage Error)**
Forecast accuracy metric weighted by volume or value, giving more importance to high-volume items.

**Working Capital**
Financial measure: Current Assets - Current Liabilities. In IBP context, often focused on inventory investment.

---

## X

**XYZ Analysis**
Classification based on demand variability. X items have stable demand (easy to forecast), Y items are moderate, Z items are highly variable.
