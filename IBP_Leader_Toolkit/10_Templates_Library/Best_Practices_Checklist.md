# IBP Best Practices Checklist

Use this checklist to assess and improve your IBP process execution.

---

## Process Design

### Overall IBP Framework
- [ ] IBP process is documented and accessible to all participants
- [ ] 24+ month planning horizon is established
- [ ] Monthly cycle follows structured calendar
- [ ] All five reviews are conducted (Product, Demand, Supply, Financial, Executive)
- [ ] Clear handoffs between reviews are defined
- [ ] Process is reviewed and updated at least annually

### Product Review
- [ ] Portfolio health metrics are tracked (vitality index, revenue mix)
- [ ] NPI pipeline is integrated into planning cycle
- [ ] SKU rationalization criteria are defined and applied
- [ ] Product lifecycle stages are clearly defined
- [ ] Market/competitive intelligence is incorporated
- [ ] Go/no-go decisions follow stage-gate process

### Demand Review
- [ ] Statistical forecasting provides baseline
- [ ] Consensus process involves Sales, Marketing, and other functions
- [ ] Assumptions are documented and tracked
- [ ] Forecast accuracy is measured at appropriate levels
- [ ] Bias is tracked and addressed
- [ ] Value-added from human judgment is measured
- [ ] Customer collaboration inputs are incorporated where applicable

### Supply Review
- [ ] Capacity is reviewed against demand plan
- [ ] Constraints are identified and logged
- [ ] Inventory targets are service-level driven
- [ ] Multiple scenarios are developed for uncertainty
- [ ] Supplier capacity is aligned with plan
- [ ] Make-vs-buy decisions are data-driven

### Financial Review
- [ ] Operating plan converts to P&L projection
- [ ] Gap analysis compares plan to budget/target
- [ ] Working capital implications are assessed
- [ ] Scenarios have financial impact quantified
- [ ] One number alignment achieved across functions

### Executive IBP
- [ ] C-suite attendance is consistent
- [ ] Meeting focuses on decisions, not presentations
- [ ] Escalated issues are resolved with clear decisions
- [ ] Actions are tracked with accountability
- [ ] Plan is formally approved each cycle
- [ ] Communication cascade is executed post-meeting

---

## Governance

### Structure
- [ ] Executive sponsor is identified and engaged
- [ ] Dedicated IBP process owner is in place
- [ ] Review owners are assigned for each review
- [ ] RACI is documented and followed
- [ ] IBP governance council meets regularly

### Meetings
- [ ] Calendar is published for full year
- [ ] Pre-reads distributed 48+ hours before meetings
- [ ] Meetings start and end on time
- [ ] Attendance expectations are clear and met
- [ ] Minutes are distributed within 24 hours
- [ ] Decision log is maintained

### Process Compliance
- [ ] Deadlines are consistently met
- [ ] Templates are standardized and used
- [ ] Data inputs are timely and accurate
- [ ] Outputs are delivered as committed
- [ ] Exceptions are documented and addressed

---

## Data & Technology

### Data Quality
- [ ] Single source of truth is established
- [ ] Master data is governed
- [ ] Data quality is measured (accuracy, completeness, timeliness)
- [ ] Historical data is clean and reliable
- [ ] Data stewards are assigned

### Systems
- [ ] Planning system is integrated with ERP
- [ ] Scenario planning is technology-enabled
- [ ] Dashboards provide real-time visibility
- [ ] Systems are user-friendly and adopted
- [ ] Integration eliminates duplicate data entry

### Analytics
- [ ] Forecasting algorithms are appropriate for demand patterns
- [ ] Root cause analysis tools are available
- [ ] Visualization supports decision-making
- [ ] Self-service analytics empower users
- [ ] Advanced analytics roadmap is defined

---

## People & Culture

### Skills & Capabilities
- [ ] Training program exists for all IBP roles
- [ ] Certification pathway is available
- [ ] Analytics skills are adequate
- [ ] Facilitation skills are developed
- [ ] Knowledge transfer is planned for key roles

### Behaviors
- [ ] Cross-functional collaboration is norm
- [ ] Data-driven decision making is valued
- [ ] Accountability is embraced
- [ ] Continuous improvement mindset exists
- [ ] Blame-free environment for forecast misses

### Change Management
- [ ] Change program supports IBP implementation
- [ ] Communication is regular and effective
- [ ] Resistance is identified and addressed
- [ ] Quick wins are celebrated
- [ ] Sustainability mechanisms are in place

---

## Performance Management

### KPIs
- [ ] IBP KPIs are defined across all perspectives
- [ ] Targets are set and communicated
- [ ] Performance is tracked at appropriate frequency
- [ ] KPIs are reviewed and actioned
- [ ] Leading indicators supplement lagging measures

### Measurement
- [ ] Forecast accuracy uses appropriate methodology
- [ ] Bias tracking identifies systematic errors
- [ ] Plan adherence is measured for demand and supply
- [ ] Plan stability is tracked
- [ ] Meeting effectiveness is surveyed

### Improvement
- [ ] Performance reviews are conducted (monthly, quarterly)
- [ ] Root cause analysis is performed for misses
- [ ] Improvement actions are tracked
- [ ] Best practices are identified and shared
- [ ] Benchmarking provides external perspective

---

## Financial Integration

### Planning Alignment
- [ ] Operating plan and financial plan are reconciled
- [ ] Gap to budget is quantified
- [ ] Gap closure initiatives are tracked
- [ ] Rolling forecast is integrated
- [ ] Working capital plan is aligned

### Decision Support
- [ ] Scenarios include financial impact
- [ ] Investment decisions have business case
- [ ] Trade-offs are financially evaluated
- [ ] Risk is quantified where possible

---

## External Collaboration

### Customers
- [ ] Strategic accounts share forecasts
- [ ] Joint business planning is conducted
- [ ] Customer intelligence informs demand
- [ ] Service commitments are aligned with capabilities

### Suppliers
- [ ] Key suppliers receive forecast visibility
- [ ] Capacity constraints are communicated both ways
- [ ] Collaborative planning improves response
- [ ] Risk is jointly managed
